Version: 1.2.0
Date: UNRELEASED
  Features:
    - New startup toggle: allows the vanilla foundation item to be placed on Fat Jelly
      ('carnova-biome1') and makes shift-placed buildings auto-fill it with foundation
      (like lava). Requires a restart.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-10-10
  Features:
    - New startup toggle: adds Apia's tile recipes (wax platform, artificial honey/copper/iron
      soil) to the Biosynthesizer. The artificial soil recipes keep their Apia surface
      restriction. Requires a restart.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-10
  Features:
    - Adds configurable module slots to the Artificial Hive (startup setting, 0-8, default 6).
    - Adds per-effect startup toggles (speed, productivity, consumption, pollution, quality) for
      the Artificial Hive. These apply both to modules inserted in the hive and to effects
      received from beacons. The productivity toggle also enables productivity on the hive's
      honeycomb recipes (they don't allow it by default).
